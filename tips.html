Mindful Breathing

Practice the 4-7-8 technique: Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7 seconds, exhale for 8 seconds. Repeat 5 times.

Nature Therapy

Spend at least 20 minutes daily in green spaces. Studies show this reduces cortisol levels by 15%.

Gratitude Journal

Write down 3 things you're grateful for every morning. This rewires your brain for positivity.

Music Therapy

Listen to calming music (60 BPM or less) for 15 minutes to lower heart rate and blood pressure.

Mindful Eating

Eat without distractions, savoring each bite. This improves digestion and reduces stress eating.

Sleep Hygiene

Maintain a consistent sleep schedule and avoid screens 1 hour before bedtime for better rest.
